🚴‍♀️ Excel Bike Sales Dashboard
This project presents a comprehensive Bike Sales Dashboard created using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard provides a clear visualization of bike purchase trends and customer demographics, helping stakeholders make informed decisions based on various factors such as age, income, commute, and family size.

📊 Dashboard Insights:
Overall Bike Purchases: Pie chart showing percentage of customers who purchased bikes.

Purchases by Age Group: Comparison of bike purchases across different age segments (Young Adult, Middle Age, Senior).

Effect of Children in Household: Analysis of how the number of children influences bike purchasing behavior.

Average Income by Gender: Bar chart showing the average income per bike purchase by gender.

Commute vs Bike Purchases: Patterns of bike purchasing based on commuting distance.

Number of Cars Owned: Relationship between the number of cars in the household and bike purchase rates.

🔍 Filters Available:
Marital Status

Home Ownership

Region (Europe, North America, Pacific)

Education Level

Occupation

📁 Dataset:
The dataset includes detailed customer information such as demographic data, income, number of children, and bike purchase status. The data has been cleaned and structured for optimal visualization in Excel.

🛠 Tools Used:
Microsoft Excel

Pivot Tables
